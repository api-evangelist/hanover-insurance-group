Article What to do if you’re caught boating in a storm It’s important to be on the lookout for bad weather before boating, but the weather can be unpredictable. In 2024 alone, bad weather was the leading cause of 224 water vessel incidents. If you find yourself on the water when a storm rolls in, quick thinking and preparation can make all the difference in returning safely. Caught in an unexpected storm? Follow these safety tips. Don’t ignore the lightning – As soon as you see lightning strike, stop any activities. Put on your life jackets – Ensure everyone on board is wearing a properly fitted life jacket. Secure loose items – Storing extra items onboard helps lower the chance of passenger injuries. Disconnect electronics – Disconnecting any extra power sources can help prevent surges from the storm. Close the windows – It may not be the first thing on your mind when a storm comes in, but it is a crucial step to avoid a flooded cabin which can weigh down the boat. Steer in a 45-degree angle – Finally, when you think you can make it to shore, going at a 45-degree angle can help to get you there as safely as possible. Stay prepared, stay protected.
